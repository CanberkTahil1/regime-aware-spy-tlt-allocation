# R4 — Robustness, costs, and the three deferred zoo rows

## In two sentences
In 1990–2019 nothing promoted in R2/R3 changes under stress. No layer flips sign at 2×/3× costs or +75 bp financing,
except v1, which trades 28× a year. The three rows you deferred (v1's spread and Bollinger signals as cash overlays, and
a gradient-boosted vol forecast) all land within ±0.07 Sharpe of the core mix, and none beats it.

## What happened, step by step
- **First, before any number:** the definitions of the three zoo rows went into `spec/prereg.yaml` `r4_zoo`, with the
  sha256 in the ledger (your O3.1 condition). Seven design questions went to the advisor (D4.1–D4.7). An eighth came up
  in the build (D4.8, the OpenMP library clash).
- `src/rspy/robust.py` does four jobs:
  - `build_core` rebuilds the whole ladder for one bond maturity, band width or calendar, checked bit for bit against
    R2/R3;
  - `overlay_keep` builds v1's signals;
  - `gbm_predict` runs LightGBM through quantkit's leakage-guarded `fit_cv`, refit each year-end;
  - `waterfall` builds the per-rung loss waterfall.
- `scripts/r4.py` → `reports/r4/`:
  - `zoo.csv`, `costs.csv` (1×/2×/3×, +75 bp), `loco.csv`, `decades.csv`, `rolling.csv`, `power.csv`, `waterfall.csv`,
    `sensitivity.csv` (41 books);
  - `dsr.csv` (787 trial series, including v1's 648-config search re-run on the new data);
  - `pbo.csv`.
- Eight expectations failed and were logged before diagnosis. None was an economic bug: my bands ignored the one-day
  round trip of the overlays, L2's daily trading, the 2000s, and float-dust financing.
- Charts: `reports/figures/R4/` (10 statistics charts) and the ignored `reports/figures_local/R4/` (`viz.headline` of
  the zoo rows and of the built rungs, rolling ΔSharpe).

## Why these choices
- D4.1 (advisor): v1's signal sends the sleeve it would sell to T-bills, on the signal day only. It is used every day,
  not just in v1's VIX state (disclosed).
- D4.2 (advisor): LightGBM predicts the next 21 days' vol. It is refit yearly with a 22-day purge and may move only
  halfway from the zoo forecast ("50% haircut"). The raw version is shown too.
- D4.3 / D4.8 (advisor): quantkit's `ml` extra at the same tag, plus quantkit's documented fix for the OpenMP clash.
- D4.4 (advisor): every built rung gets the same stress tests, because nothing was promoted.
- D4.5 (advisor): maturity, band and calendar are changed one at a time.
- D4.6 (advisor): every registered trial plus v1's grid counts toward the DSR.
- D4.7 (advisor): PBO over the zoo, 16 blocks.

## The one number to remember
**87 years**: how much daily data it would take to confirm, with 80% power, even inverse-vol's measured +0.13 Sharpe
edge over 60/40. That assumes the 1990–2019 estimate is the truth; the sample is 30 years.

## Concept check
- **Effective number of trials:** 787 backtests, but they are all long-only SPY/TLT mixes, so at correlation 0.5 they
  form about 3 independent bets. The DSR then compares each Sharpe with the expected best of about 4 null trials
  (SR0 ≈ 0.19). Counting all 748 registered configs raw gives SR0 ≈ 0.37, and v1's DSR falls from 0.96 to 0.78. The
  truth is between the two.
- **Power:** years ≈ (z₀.₉₅ + z₀.₈)² · V / ΔSR², with V = 2(1 − ρ) + ½(SR₁² + SR₂² − 2SR₁SR₂ρ²) per period. High
  correlation between a layer and its parent (ρ ≈ 0.9–0.99) helps, but small gaps (0.01–0.13) still need decades.

## What could be wrong
The DSR pool's effective count (3) comes from clustering raw returns at ρ* 0.5. That is quantkit's default, but for
strategies that all hold the same two assets it barely deflates anything. The raw-N column (748) is the other bound, and
the honest answer lies between them. Also, the 10-year-bond result (+0.07 to +0.10 Sharpe for every bond rung) uses a
GSW constant-maturity proxy never checked against an ETF, and it was not a registered choice. It is a lead for later,
not a finding.
