# R2 — The core mix (L2–L5)

## In two sentences
On 1990–2019, holding the SPY proxy and the TLT proxy (25-year Treasury rebuilt from the Fed curve) together, weighted
by inverse volatility, beat SPY + T-bills at the same risk by +0.24 to +0.26 excess Sharpe (95% intervals include zero),
with worst drawdowns of −18% and −13% against −43% for v1 — in a 30-year bond bull market that flatters a long-duration
bond leg. Neither promotion walk promotes it: the pre-registered walk stopped at vol-targeted SPY vs v1, and the
post-hoc parent-chain walk you chose (O2.3) stopped at 60/40 vs SPY, which clears the effect and significance tests
(+0.21, p 0.005) but has a lower Sharpe than SPY inside the 1994 bond crash and Q4 2018.

## The one number to remember
**+0.26 excess Sharpe**: inverse-vol + vol target (L5) over SPY + T-bills at the same volatility, 1990–2019 daily
(95% interval −0.04 to +0.55, one-sided p 0.04). In-sample, descriptive — not a promotion.

## Concept check
- **Matched-volatility comparison.** Sharpe ignores leverage, so the fair benchmark is SPY + bills mixed to the same
  risk: w_t = σ̂(strategy) / σ̂(SPY − bill) over the last 63 days, known the day before.
- **Two-asset ERC = inverse vol.** Risk contributions w₁(Σw)₁ = w₂(Σw)₂ reduce to w₁σ₁ = w₂σ₂; the correlation term
  cancels.

## Interview questions
1. *Your stock-bond mix looks far better than v1 — why didn't it get promoted?* Two walks, neither promoted it. The
   pre-registered one judged 60/40 after vol-targeted SPY and stopped earlier; the parent-chain walk judged 60/40 against
   SPY, where it cleared the effect and significance tests but had a lower Sharpe inside two crisis windows, and the
   rule demands no loss in any. Overriding a rule after seeing it bind is exactly what pre-registration forbids.
2. *How do you compare a 8%-vol portfolio with SPY at 17%?* Against SPY + T-bills scaled to the portfolio's own trailing
   63-day volatility, using only data available the day before; the full-sample exact match is shown only for reference.
3. *Why does the vol target barely matter here?* The inverse-vol mix already runs below 10% vol on 84% of days, so the
   1.0× cap binds and the layer only acts in high-vol spells: +0.01 Sharpe and −7% CDaR at matched vol.

## What could be wrong
The chain walk was chosen after seeing R2's results, so it is labelled post-hoc and counted as an extra trial; it
promotes nothing, so the post-hoc choice did not flatter the result. Known limitation (O2.4): the crisis check compares
Sharpe ratios inside each window, and when both books lose money the calmer one can "lose" while losing less — it
happens to vol-targeted SPY in the GFC (−27% vs −41% for SPY at the same risk). A descriptive side-row
(reports/r2/crisis_matched.csv) redoes each window as cumulative return with the parent scaled to the same risk: 60/40
still loses 1994 (−6.9% vs −0.3%) and, narrowly, Q4 2018 (−7.7% vs −7.4%), so its failure is not an artefact. The
stock-bond numbers are point estimates from 1990–2019, when falling yields paid a 25-year bond leg handsomely, and the
bond proxy cannot be checked against TLT before 2005. R3 tests its new layers through the ablation table, not the walk.
