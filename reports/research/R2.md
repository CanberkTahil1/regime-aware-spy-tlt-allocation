## D2.1 — Vol-target mechanics and the no-trade band (L2, L4, L5)
- (a) [rec] Daily vol-target decisions (forecast stamped t, target set at close t, traded t+1: charter "close t (daily
  vol target)"); monthly weight decisions at the month-end close; one **drift-aware 5 pp per-sleeve no-trade band**
  (the charter's L3 number, no new parameter) applied inside `rspy.accounting` to every rung with a band: at a decision
  row, a sleeve whose drifted weight is within ±5 pp of its target is not traded; outside, it trades to the band edge
  (de Lataillade et al.; quantkit's `band` convention). Needed because `quantkit.trade.rules.band` compares to the last
  target, not the drifted weight; reference loop + equivalence test.
- (b) As (a) but trade to the target when outside the band (Vanguard threshold rebalancing). Fewer, larger trades.
- (c) Monthly vol scaling only (Moreira & Muir form): one trade per month, no daily layer. Contradicts the charter's
  "close t (daily vol target)" wording for L5.
- (d) Daily scaling with no band. Turnover in the tens per year on equities (Harvey et al.) [mem].
- Pushback: a band changes what "vol target" means (realised vol drifts up to the band) — yes; the band is the cost
  control the charter's 5 pp already accepts for L3, and true turnover is still charged.

## D2.2 — Which series the vol models forecast, and how the zoo model is chosen
- (a) [rec] Univariate forecasts of the **unscaled parent book's own daily net return** (L2: SPY proxy; L5: the L4
  book), h = 1, log returns, every zoo model (EWMA hl 10 "fast", EWMA hl 60 "slow", GJR-GARCH(1,1)-t, Beta-t-EGARCH
  (quantkit's EGARCH-t), HAR on squared daily returns, all quantkit defaults: window 1000, refit every 21, anchor 500).
  Selection at each year-end y (1989 … 2018) for year y+1: QLIKE against r²_{t+1} on all rows ≤ y-end with every model
  defined (expanding); 90% MCS (`quantkit.vol.mcs`, block 21, 1,000 reps, seed 0); the **simplest model inside the MCS**
  in the fixed order EWMA-slow, EWMA-fast, HAR, GJR-GARCH-t, Beta-t-EGARCH (simple wins ties). Each model also runs as
  a registered zoo row (its own L5 variant).
- (b) As (a) but pick the lowest mean QLIKE (no MCS, no simplicity order).
- (c) Per-sleeve forecasts + 63-day correlation → wᵀΣw. Uses the zoo per asset; needs a covariance forecast the zoo
  lacks (ad hoc mix).
- (d) Fix one model (EWMA hl 10, RiskMetrics) for the rung; zoo descriptive only. Simplest; ignores "chosen by
  rolling QLIKE / MCS" in BUILD_PROMPTS.
- Pushback: QLIKE on h = 1 rewards day-ahead accuracy while the band holds positions longer — accepted; h = 1 is
  quantkit's `vol_target` convention and Patton-robust.

## D2.3 — What L2 is
- (a) [rec] L2 = vol-targeted SPY with **v1's own scaling** (15% target, 20-day realised vol, cap 2.0, daily), now
  financed at bill + 25 bp and costed, i.e. "v1's real driver, isolated" (charter); descriptive companion L2-u = SPY at
  10% ex ante, cap 1.0, through the D2.2 machinery (same vol layer as L5, so L5 − L4 and L2-u − L0 share a definition).
- (b) L2 = L2-u only (10%, cap 1.0, zoo-selected). Cleaner layer comparison; loses the "v1's driver" isolation.
- (c) L2 = v1's scaling unlevered (cap 1.0). Mixes the two.
- Pushback: a 2× cap rung is levered; it is judged at matched vol and pays financing, so leverage alone cannot win.

## D2.4 — Promotion rule implementation
- (a) [rec] Copy naive-vs-optimal's pattern (COPIED.md): `Ladder.promote` on **daily bill-excess returns rescaled ex
  post to a common 10% vol** (constant per rung over development: Sharpe and the LW test are unchanged by a constant;
  the MCS becomes "on vol-matched returns", charter) with metric sharpe, test lw, min_effect 0.05, alpha 0.05,
  cost_mults (1, 2), stress windows = the six development crisis windows, mcs 0.90, reps 1,000, mean_block 21,
  seed 0, knowledge date 2019-12-31; AND rspy legs: CDaR route (CDaR-95 of the candidate ≤ 0.85 × the incumbent's,
  incumbent rescaled ex post to the candidate's vol, financed above 1×), +75 bp financing (stress books), LOCO over the
  six crises. Effect = Sharpe route OR CDaR route; robust legs per route as the sibling. Walk L1 → L2 → L3 → L4 → L5
  (fixed sequence, stop at first failure); L0/L0-line benchmarks; L4b, L5-lev, L2-u and zoo rows descriptive.
- (b) quantkit ladder only (Sharpe route, its in-window stress); report CDaR, +75 bp and LOCO descriptively. Breaks
  the charter's "or CDaR" and robustness list.
- (c) Monthly grid (as the sibling, block 12). Fewer rows (360), loses daily vol-target information.
- Pushback: the fixed sequence means a failed L3 (60/40 vs vol-targeted SPY) stops L4/L5 being tested — yes, by the
  owner's quantkit rule; every rung still gets a descriptive verdict vs L1 and vs the line.

## D2.5 — Tranche offsets and the rung of record
- (a) [rec] Rung of record = month-end decision (charter "month-end close t"). Timing luck: `quantkit.trade.tranche`
  over **all 21 trading-day offsets (0–20)** for L3, L4, L5 (monthly weight decision moves; daily vol layer unchanged),
  tranched book and RTL reported (`timing_luck`), never selected from.
- (b) K = 4 weekly offsets {0, 5, 10, 15}. Cheaper, coarser.
- (c) Tranched book as the rung of record. Smoother but not the charter's execution rule.

## D2.6 — L4b (ERC)
- (a) [rec] `quantkit.alloc.risk.erc` on the 63-day sample covariance; record the two-asset identity ERC = inverse
  vol (checked 8.8e-15) and a test; L4b reported as identical to L4, one registered zoo row.
- (b) `erc_lw` (Ledoit-Wolf shrinkage toward μI): differs from L4 only through shrunk variances.
- (c) Drop L4b. The charter lists it.

## D2.7 — L4 inverse-vol estimator
- (a) [rec] σ_i = sample std (ddof 1) of the 63 daily returns ending at the decision close (charter "63-day vol");
  O(T) rolling; weights ∝ 1/σ_i, Σw = 1.
- (b) `quantkit.alloc.risk.inverse_vol` (EWMA hl 12): not the charter's 63-day window.

## D2.8 — The L0-line
- (a) [rec] `quantkit.bench.vol_matched(rung, hi20, bill, mode="ex_ante", window=63, lag=1, financing=bill MMY +
  25 bp)` per rung as the judged line; the ex-post line (full-sample σ, descriptive) shown beside it.
- (b) Ex-post only (exact vol match, look-ahead constant; Cederburg et al. warn).
- (c) Fixed 60-day window. No source for 60 over quantkit's 63 default.
